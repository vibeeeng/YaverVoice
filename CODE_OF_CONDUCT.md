# Code of Conduct

YaverVoice follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, insults, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate are not accepted in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `vibeeeng@gmail.com`. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may correct, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants depending on impact and recurrence. Enforcement decisions may be reconsidered when new evidence is available.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
