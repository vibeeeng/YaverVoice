# Product

## Register

product

## Users

YaverVoice is for Windows and Linux users who need to turn microphone recordings or existing audio and video files into usable text. They may work through repeated desktop workflows and need recording, transcription, notes, conversion, and transcript management to remain fast, legible, and dependable without demanding unnecessary attention.

## Product Purpose

YaverVoice provides a focused desktop workspace for microphone capture, file transcription, current-session transcript management, Markdown document generation, audio conversion, settings, and system readiness. Success means users can complete Capture, Files, Library, Docs, Converter, and Settings workflows with low cognitive load while always understanding the current operation, its progress, and whether Groq Cloud or Local Whisper is processing transcription data. Cloud and local processing must remain visibly distinct, and the interface must communicate where data is processed whenever that distinction matters.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, creative, and reassuring. YaverVoice should feel soft yet distinctive: expressive enough to be memorable, disciplined enough to remain a trustworthy productivity tool, and calm enough for sustained desktop use.

## Anti-references

YaverVoice must not resemble a generic, sterile utility; a neon or gamer interface; an over-decorated concept product; or an oversized, sparsely populated dashboard. Effects must not overwhelm content, color must not carry meaning alone, and visual character must never obscure workflow state or control affordances.

## Design Principles

1. **Protect the workflow.** Preserve every existing feature, control, state, and provider boundary; improve how users find and understand them rather than changing what they do.
2. **Compact without crowding.** Reduce wasteful space while keeping hierarchy, reading comfort, and interaction targets strong enough for sustained desktop use.
3. **Make processing explicit.** Pair color with labels, icons, or supporting text so users can identify status, progress, the active transcription provider, and whether data is processed locally or in the cloud.
4. **Build character with restraint.** Treat the current turquoise-led palette as a starting point, not an immutable limit. Evaluate a compatible secondary accent through visual alternatives, and add it only when it has a defined role, preserves semantic clarity and AA contrast, and strengthens the energetic, creative character without neon spectacle or decorative noise.
5. **Keep the tool trustworthy.** Maintain consistent controls, predictable navigation, legible information density, and clear feedback across Capture, Files, Library, Docs, Converter, and Settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Provide sufficient contrast, visible keyboard focus, complete keyboard access, appropriately sized click targets, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives while preserving balanced compactness. Do not use color as the sole indicator of state or meaning.
